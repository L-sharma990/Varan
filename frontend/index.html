FAQ

Common Questions

How does the ₹1 mock payment work?
A simulated ₹1 payment is processed during registration. If you Exit without freezing a seat, it's automatically refunded in our logs.
What do Freeze, Float, and Exit mean?
Freeze: Lock seat, exit future rounds. Float: Keep seat, eligible for upgrades. Exit: Surrender seat, get refund.
Can I change my choices between rounds?
Yes! As long as the admin has opened choice filling, you can add, remove, or reorder preferences from your dashboard.
How is merit evaluated?
Seats are allocated strictly by JEE Main Rank (ascending). Better ranks are processed first and awarded their highest available preference.
